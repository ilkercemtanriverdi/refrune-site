One-line description

Refrune is a poker solitaire puzzle for iPhone where every line of a 5x5 gem board scores twice, as a number hand and as a color hand.

Description

Each gem in Refrune has a number from 1 to 5 and a color. You place 25 of them on a 5x5 board, and every row, column and diagonal is scored twice: once as a poker-style number hand (pair, straight, full house, five of a kind) and once as a color hand (flush, rainbow). A line that makes a perfect straight can be a terrible color hand, so every placement is a trade-off between the two.

The campaign has 30 handcrafted levels across five worlds, each adding its own twist. Around it sit a daily puzzle with a Game Center leaderboard, Duel (two players on the same puzzle), pass-and-play for 2-4 players on one phone, an 8-floor Expedition where each floor adds a new curse, and free play. Poker hands are only scoring patterns: there is no betting, no currency and no prizes.

Features

  • Dual scoring: number hands and color hands on every line
  • 30 handcrafted levels, first 12 free
  • Daily puzzle with Game Center leaderboard
  • Duel and 2-4 player pass-and-play
  • Expedition: 8 floors, each with a new curse
  • Glass and marimba sound sets, haptics, VoiceOver labels
  • Fully offline, no accounts, no ads, no tracking
