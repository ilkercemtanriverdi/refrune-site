Refrune: Gem Poker Puzzle

Poker solitaire with gems, for iPhone · Türkçe

Download on the App Store

Refrune is a poker solitaire puzzle game for iPhone. You place 25 gems on a 5x5 board. Every gem has a number from 1 to 5 and a color. Each row, column and diagonal scores twice: once as a number hand (pair, two pair, three of a kind, straight, full house, four or five of a kind) and once as a color hand (such as flush or rainbow). Every gem you place changes what the next one is worth, so the game is about planning both hands at once.

Features

  • 30 handcrafted levels, each with its own twist. The first 12 are free forever.
  • One-time USD 3.99 unlock for levels 13-30. No ads, no subscriptions.
  • Daily puzzle with a Game Center leaderboard.
  • Duel: two players on the same puzzle. Pass-and-play for 2-4 players on one phone.
  • Expedition: climb 8 floors, each adding a new curse (a roguelike-style challenge).
  • Free play with random gems, glass and marimba sound sets, haptics, VoiceOver labels.
  • Plays offline. No accounts, no tracking, no data collected. English and Turkish.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of game is Refrune?
A poker solitaire puzzle: poker-style hands on a 5x5 grid, scored for numbers and colors at the same time.

Is it gambling?
No. Poker hand rankings are only scoring patterns. There is no betting, no currency and no prizes.

Is it free?
Levels 1-12, the daily puzzle and all modes are free. A single USD 3.99 purchase unlocks levels 13-30.

Does it work offline?
Yes. Game Center leaderboards are optional.

Who makes Refrune?
Refrune is made by independent developer İlker Cem Tanrıverdi.
